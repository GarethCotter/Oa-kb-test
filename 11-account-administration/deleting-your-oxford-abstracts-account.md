# Deleting your Oxford Abstracts account

## Easily delete your Oxford Abstracts Account by following the simple steps below.

If you no longer wish to have your Oxford Abstracts account you can now delete it from your account settings.

  

**Please note:** All content you have uploaded (e.g. submissions) will be automatically deleted and unable to be retrieved once you delete your account.

  

To delete your account, from your dashboard **click on your email address** at the t**op right of the page.**

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2024-09-18-at-12-09-02-png-03bf51ce.webp)  
  

Select **My Profile,** **scroll down to Account,** and **Click on Delete Account** and follow the instructions.

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2024-09-18-at-12-09-08-png-2454a8b8.webp)  
  

A box will appear on the next screen that you will need to **type in DELETE** and **click on Confirm Deletion** to delete your account.![](/assets/img/screenshot-2024-10-03-at-10-40-33-png-4f95acc2.webp)

Please note if you have uploaded content (an abstract etc) then you may receive the following instructions on the screenshot below.

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2024-10-03-at-10-42-13-png-3c008ae9.webp)

If this happens, please contact the Event Administrator of the event your account is linked to so they can delete it.

Should you need further assistance, please contact our Support Desk via this [Contact Form.](https://oxfordabstracts.com/resources/contact-support/)
