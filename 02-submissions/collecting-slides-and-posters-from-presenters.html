Collecting slides and posters from your presenters

There is no separate upload area for presentations — you collect them through the submission form, which means there are two decisions to get right before you ask anyone for anything.#

Add a file upload question#

Go to Event dashboard → Abstract Management → Submissions → Forms & Setup → +QUESTION and add a file upload question. Set the size limit to suit what you are asking for: it starts at 10MB, which is nowhere near enough for a slide deck full of images, and you can raise it to 500MB. See Submission question types.

Tick the Program setting on the question if you want what they upload to appear against their session in the programme. This is the step people miss, and the symptom is files that arrive perfectly well but never show up anywhere public.

One question per file. There is no multi-file upload, so if you want slides and a poster and a video, that is three questions.

Decide how they will get back in#

This is where it usually goes wrong. If you are asking accepted submitters to add something after the fact, their submission is already complete — and if your form is open for incomplete submissions only, they cannot edit it, so they cannot attach anything either.

You have two options:

  • Reopen the form for complete submissions. Simplest, and fine if you are happy for them to change the rest of their submission too. See Opening and closing submissions (deadlines).
  • Collect it in a second stage. Better if you want the original locked. Put only the upload question on the new stage and hide or fix the rest. See Setting up a multi-stage event.

Only the submitter can upload#

A submission belongs to whoever created it. Co-authors, panellists and additional presenters cannot add their own files — they do not have access to the submission at all.

For a panel where several people each have slides, you have two honest choices: the submitter collects the files and uploads them on everyone's behalf, or each panellist makes their own submission, which means separate submission numbers to reconcile afterwards. There is no third option, so decide before you announce it.

If somebody is both an administrator and a presenter, check which account their submission actually sits under — being signed in as an admin does not give you edit rights over your own submission if it was made with a different address.

Getting the files back out#

Collected files come down as a zipped folder from Reports & Downloads, under the question response folders — see Downloading all submissions and the files attached to them.

Tell people the limit up front#

Put the file size limit and the accepted formats in the question text. Presenters discover the limit at the moment they try to upload, usually the night before the deadline, and that is when they email you instead.
