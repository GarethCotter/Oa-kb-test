# Letting submitters hide their abstract from the programme

## This option lets submitters choose whether or not their abstract / poster is displayed in the program.

The guidance below is for event administrators/ organisers. If you are an end user (eg. submitter, reviewer, delegate etc), please click [here](https://help.oxfordabstracts.com/knowledge/support?hsLang=en).

Go to **[Event dashboard](/01-getting-started/event-dashboard) → Abstract Management → Submission → Form & Setup.**

  

The [submission form](/02-submissions/designing-the-submission-form) will then be displayed.

Clicking on the **+ Question** button at the top of the edit view of the submission form will take you to [Question types](/02-submissions/submission-question-types).

  
![](/assets/img/undefined-mar-04-2021-03-32-34-05-pm-aae92baf.webp)  

At the bottom of the list of questions, you will see the option below - **Program permission**

  
![](/assets/img/s3-amazonaws-comcdn-freshdesk-comdatahelpdeskattachmentsprod-0d7c2d53.png)

Select it, and click **C**reate selected question**, at the top.

  

Fill in the details to your requirements and click **Create Question**

  
  
![](/assets/img/s3-amazonaws-comcdn-freshdesk-comdatahelpdeskattachmentsprod-acafdf32.webp)  

When submitters complete the form, the question will display as below:

![](/assets/img/s3-amazonaws-comcdn-freshdesk-comdatahelpdeskattachmentsprod-094986b0.png)  

If they do not check the box, it won't be possible to view their abstract and the following message will appear:

![](/assets/img/s3-amazonaws-comcdn-freshdesk-comdatahelpdeskattachmentsprod-3225d12e.png)  
![](/assets/img/oxfordabstracts-freshdesk-comsupportsolutionsarticles8000081-ea21dd73.gif)
