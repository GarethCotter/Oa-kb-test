If your poster gallery is empty

Work through the six things that stop a poster appearing, in the order they are most likely.#

You have added a poster question, your submitters have uploaded their posters, and the gallery still says "Sorry, we couldn't find any posters".

The gallery offers you a Clear filters button, but a filter is rarely the reason. A poster only appears when several separate things are all true, and the gallery cannot tell you which one is missing. Work down this list in order — the first two causes account for most cases.

1. The submissions are ticked but not accepted#

This is the most common cause by a distance.

Ticking In poster gallery in the decisions table is not enough on its own. The submission must also have a decision of Accepted. A submission that is still Pending will not appear, however many boxes you tick.

Go to Event dashboard → Abstract Management → Decisions → Table and check the Decision column for the posters you expect to see.

Note

Accepted: Poster is a decision type, not a gallery setting. Choosing it does not add anything to the poster gallery. You still need the In poster gallery box ticked as well.

2. No question is marked as the poster gallery upload#

The poster gallery is created by a single question on your submission form. If no question has Poster gallery upload (1 allowed per event) ticked, there is no gallery to fill — and the In poster gallery column will not appear in your decisions table at all.

If you cannot find that column, this is why.

Go to Event dashboard → Abstract Management → Submissions → Form & setup, open your poster question, and check that Poster gallery upload (1 allowed per event) is ticked. If there is no poster question at all, click + QUESTION and choose Poster upload under Poster questions. See Professional Conference - the poster gallery for the full setup.

Tip

Decisions has a Form & setup page too. The poster question is on the one under Submissions.

3. Your submitters have not uploaded anything#

A submission with no file attached to the poster question will not appear, even if it is accepted and ticked.

In the decisions table, click COLUMNS, type poster in the search box, and tick Poster under Submission responses. That column shows you who has actually uploaded a file.

4. The Poster gallery menu item is switched off#

Go to Event dashboard → Conference → Program → Builder → DISPLAY → Program menu and check that Poster gallery is ticked. It is ticked by default.

The name in that box can be edited, so if someone has renamed it, the menu item in your programme will show the new name rather than "Poster gallery".

5. Submission contents are hidden from the programme#

Your programme's access settings include a Show submission contents option. Unticking it hides all submission data from the programme, including the whole poster gallery. It is ticked by default, so this only applies if someone has changed it.

See Controlling access to the programme.

6. A filter is still applied#

If you have typed something into Search posters, or ticked anything in the Topics or Keywords dropdowns, you are looking at a filtered view. Click Clear filters to see everything.

Note

The search box searches poster titles and author affiliations. It does not search abstracts or poster keywords. To find posters by keyword, use the Keywords dropdown instead.

Still empty?#

If you have worked through all six and your gallery is still empty, contact support with your event ID and the ID of one submission you expect to see. That is enough for us to check it directly.
