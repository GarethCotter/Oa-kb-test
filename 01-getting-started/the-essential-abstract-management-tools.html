The essential abstract management tools

Getting startedLast reviewed 28 July 2026

For event organisersNot an organiser?

On this pageThe essential toolsSubmissionReviewingDecisionsCommunication

A brief introduction to the features of the abstract management toolbox, designed to help you manage the submission, review and decision process for your academic or research conference.#

Note

The features highlighted in pink are NOT available (or are limited) in the FREE package. The FREE package also features Oxford Abstracts branding throughout.

The essential tools#

Submission#

  • Collect unlimited abstracts
  • Embed secure online submission forms on your website or in emails
  • Choose from an extensive range of fully customisable question templates
  • Infinite range of options to search, filter and download submissions
  • Submitters can add content, and upload supporting documents in any file format, e.g. PDF, PowerPoint
  • You can submit on behalf of a user

  • Manage, view, delete, withdraw and edit submissions with ease on our intuitive interface

  • Editable headers and footers in submission form

  • Add IEEE copyright to submission form
  • Charge fees for submission
  • Restricting who can submit by email
  • Author / presenter specific forms

Reviewing#

  • Collect the review data you need for grading, comments and typical review functions
  • Assign reviewers to submissions, or submissions to reviewers, individually or in bulk
  • Clean, easy-to-navigate reviewer screen for simple, stress-free reviewer experience
  • Monitor complete and incomplete reviews, with built-in follow up process
  • Infinite range of options to search, view, filter and download reviews

  • Reviewers can record a conflict of interest

  • Fully editable range of template questions

  • Show reviews to submitters

Decisions#

  • Add acceptance types, questions and a range of input options
  • Control the visibility of data for your committee
  • Find data instantly with a multi-filter and search-friendly table

  • Accept, reject and withdraw submissions, with integrated manual and automatic email notification

  • Semi - editable range of template questions

  • Assigning specific categories to committee members.

Communication#

  • Add admins, committee members, reviewers, API users with just their email address
  • Schedule emails, or send manually
  • Track email delivery

  • Send individually, or in bulk

  • Custom creation of automatic and manual emails for streamlined communication with submitters and all collaborators

  • Add signatures to emails
  • Add extra notification emails - a great solution if you need to cc emails to a secondary address

Publishing and reporting

  • Create real-time abstract books from default templates
  • Create real-time reports from default templates
  • Filter and arrange submission, review and decision data for Excel report downloads
  • Live updates for real-time downloads, any time you choose

  • Quick click for indexes and country reports

  • Extensive range of abstract book options with formatting tools

  • Extensive range of report options - include any data from anywhere in the event
  • Event theme - add logos and custom colours to forms
