---
title: "Attendees cannot change their own registration answers — the organiser has to amend them"
internal: true
last_reviewed: 2026-07-31
---

Attendee wants to change their registration, edit my registration answers, change my
dietary requirements, wrong ticket type selected, amend a delegate's details, can
delegates update their own booking, registrant needs to correct their form.

**No.** Once someone has registered, they cannot go back and change the answers they
gave on the registration form. The organiser has to amend the registration on their
behalf, from the registrations table on the event dashboard.

This is a known pain point rather than a setting that is switched off somewhere — it
was raised with the product team in 2026 and is not on the roadmap as of mid-2026,
though the registration module is being looked at. Organisers who ask are usually
spending real time on it, so it is worth saying that the amendment is quick to do
from the table rather than only saying no.

**Two things that are different and do work:**

- **Their own name and email address** — attendees change those themselves from their
  profile page, and admins cannot do it for them.
- **Adding a question after people have registered** — allowed, but the new question
  is not asked of anyone who registered before it existed.
