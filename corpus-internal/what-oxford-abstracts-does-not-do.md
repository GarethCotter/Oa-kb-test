Visa letter, visa invitation letter, letter of invitation, invitation letter for
attendees, can the system generate visa letters, certificate of participation,
attendance letter, anonymous abstract book, hide author names from the abstract book.

Answer these plainly and immediately. A clear no saves a round trip; a vague answer
produces a second ticket. Where there is a real alternative, give it in the same
breath.

**Visa letters and letters of invitation: no.** The system does not generate them, and
this has to be arranged outside Oxford Abstracts. Organisers usually produce them from
their own templates using an export of accepted submitters or registered attendees, so
point them at exporting the list rather than leaving them with a flat no.

**Certificates of participation: yes, but as a paid add-on.** Do not confuse this with
visa letters. Certificates is bought separately and is included with Professional —
see the certificates content, and send pricing questions to the pricing page.

**An abstract book without author names: no.** Author details always appear in an
abstract book and cannot be suppressed. Anyone wanting anonymised material for blind
reviewing should use the reviewing side of the system, where what a reviewer can see is
controlled properly.

**Merging two accounts: no such feature** — see the account and email changes note.

**Partial refunds: not directly** — see the refund article for the two-step method.

**Only the submitter can edit a submission.** Co-authors and panellists cannot upload
files or make changes, however much the organiser wants them to.

A note on the ticket data behind this: the "visa" theme in the gap analysis is mostly
mislabelled. Most of those tickets are about **Visa cards** — expired cards, blocked
payments, "too many card attempts" — and not about visas at all. Card and payment
problems are a registration and payments question; treat them separately.
