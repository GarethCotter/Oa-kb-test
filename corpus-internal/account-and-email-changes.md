Email failed to save, cannot change email address, duplicate account, merge two
accounts, two accounts same person, lost access to old institutional email, wrong
email on a submission, submitted under the wrong account.

Distilled from 19 support replies. The public half — why the change is refused, and
what the reader can do — is on **Updating your profile**. This note is the part that
only support can act on, so answers should point people at raising a ticket rather
than describe the mechanics.

**There is no account merge feature.** What actually happens is that submissions are
moved from one account to the other and the spare account is deleted. Answer in terms
of "your submissions can be moved to the account you want to keep", never "we will
merge your accounts", which promises a button that does not exist.

**The cause of "email failed to save" is almost always that the target address already
has an account.** Worth stating plainly, because readers reasonably assume it is a
browser or blocker problem and waste time on that.

**Reviews are not straightforward to move between accounts.** Submissions are. If a
reviewer has completed reviews under the wrong address, the practical fix is usually
to move the submissions rather than the reviews, and to accept some rework.

**An event administrator can change the email address held against a submission
themselves**, from the submissions table. For anyone tied to a single conference this
is faster than coming to us, so route them to their organiser first.

**Where the person has lost access to the old mailbox**, they cannot prove the change
by email, so this always needs support. Expect to confirm both the old and new address
before anything moves.

Do not describe temporary passwords, admin URLs or the internal steps for deleting an
account in an answer. The right answer is always: contact support, say which account
to keep, and it will be sorted.
