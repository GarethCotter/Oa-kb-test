Paying by card, credit card, payment link, offline payment, mark as paid, Authorize.net
pending, registration not showing, invoice wrong name, trial expired, registration
error, special characters in address.

- **Service fee on refunds:** Oxford Abstracts does not reimburse the service fee if an
  order is refunded.
- **Submitting is not registering:** submitting an abstract does not register someone for
  the event. They must buy a ticket separately.
- **Payment links** are sent to the ticket buyer and any other recipients of the invoice,
  by email, and can also be reached from the ticket buyer's dashboard.
- **Printing a delegate list with addresses:** from the Registrations tab, download the
  table data with the download button, then manipulate and print as needed.
- **Editing the registration form after delegates have submitted:** yes, you can — but
  questions added afterwards will not appear for people who already submitted.
- **Authorize.net errors:** direct these to the support team.
- **PCI / attestation of compliance:** support can email this on request.

From a year of support replies, July 2026:

- **Paying by card:** organisers do not need a payment link from support. From the
  event dashboard, click the card icon and choose the card payment method. You have to
  be signed in as the account that owns the event.
- **The "offline payment" option** means the money moves outside Oxford Abstracts
  entirely. Once it lands in your own account, the organiser marks that registration as
  **Paid** by hand. Registrations sitting unpaid are usually this, not a fault.
- **Authorize.net payments held or pending** do not appear in the registrations table
  at all, because Authorize handles payments differently from invoices and payment
  links. Workaround: create the registration using the offline invoice option, then mark
  it paid once the money clears. The registrant gets an email and completes the form.
- **A trial that has expired** cannot be reactivated without payment — but nothing is
  lost. Website-builder work and event setup are all still there and reappear as soon
  as the upgrade goes through. Say this early; the fear of losing work is what drives
  the ticket.
- **Registration failing with an odd error** is often the address field: very long
  addresses, and commas inside them, can break it. Ask them to shorten it and remove
  commas before escalating.
- **Editing an invoice or receipt** (wrong name, wrong organisation) has to be done by
  Oxford Abstracts support — organisers cannot. Once support has amended it, download
  the corrected copy from the registrations table.
