Multi stage, second stage, rename a stage, change the stage title, move submissions
between stages, multi-stage add-on cost, is multi stage switched on, full paper stage
after abstracts, two-stage review.

Distilled from support replies in the year to July 2026.

- **Renaming a stage** is done on the **Event details** page of the event dashboard,
  at any time. Organisers hunt for it inside the stage itself and do not find it.
- **Moving submissions between stages** is done from the **Decisions** screen, not the
  submissions table.
- **Multi-stage is a separately purchased add-on.** It can be switched off, and an
  event that had it last year may not have it now — worth checking before treating
  "the stages have disappeared" as a fault. Support enables it once purchased.
- **Reviewer data does not travel between stages.** A second-stage reviewer cannot see
  what the first-stage reviewers wrote; only event admins can. If the committee needs
  that continuity, an admin has to carry it across by hand.
- **A second round of reviews on moved submissions** works normally once the
  submissions are in the new stage.
