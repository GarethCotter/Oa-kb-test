---
title: "Payment overdue on screen while the invoice says otherwise — two different dates"
internal: true
last_reviewed: 2026-07-31
---

Payment overdue message, invoice due date does not match, locked out after upgrading,
features unavailable while waiting for payment, extend the payment deadline, paying by
invoice removed my access, event says payment overdue.

**They are two separate dates and they are not linked.** The event has a payment due
date that controls access to paid features; the invoice has its own due date, and an
invoice can be regenerated at any time. So a screen can say payment is overdue while
the invoice in the organiser's hand says it is not due until next week. This is not a
billing error and not a fault.

**What to do:** support extends the event's payment due date to match the invoice.
That restores access immediately. Organisations waiting on a finance department or a
purchase order should ask for the extension rather than hurrying the payment — this is
routine and support does it readily.

**Nothing is lost while access is restricted.** Website-builder work, event setup and
submissions are all still there and reappear the moment payment or an extension goes
through. Say this early: the fear of losing work is what turns a billing question into
an urgent ticket.

**Related but different:** an expired *trial* cannot be reactivated without payment,
though the work on it is likewise still there afterwards.
