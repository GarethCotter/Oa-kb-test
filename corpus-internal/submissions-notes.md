Name shows differently in the table, username versus submission form name, wrong name
on a submission, file type and size limits on uploads, submission in two sessions has
no time, numbering.

- **"Incomplete registration"** means the user has not filled out and submitted everything
  required by the submission form.
- **"Submission has closed" when following the link:** check the submissions toggle is open
  on your dashboard. If it still happens, contact support.
- **No submission limit on the free version.**
- **Images do not count** toward the abstract word count.
- **Downloading a submission as PDF** is available on paid packages, from the
  Reports & Downloads tab.
- **Only the submitter can edit** their abstract — authors who are not the submitter
  cannot.
- **Controlling edits after acceptance:** use the submission status toggles on your event
  dashboard to decide whether submitters can keep editing.

From a year of support replies, July 2026:

- **The name in the table is not the name on the submission.** The table shows the
  *username* the person gave when they registered their account; the submission form
  holds whatever they typed into the form. The two often differ — most visibly where
  someone has entered their name in a non-Latin script — and it is not a fault. Admins
  cannot change the username; the person updates it themselves under My Profile.
- **File type and size limits on uploads** are per question type and are documented on
  the submission question types guide, under multimedia questions.
- **A submission scheduled in more than one session** only carries individual start and
  end times in the session where it has actually been timed. Seeing blank times against
  a poster that also appears in a teaser session is expected, not a scheduling bug.
