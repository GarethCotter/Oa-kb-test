Who are the conference delegates, what is a delegate, difference between delegate and
attendee, what is a registrant, what is a submitter, what is a referee, grader, judge,
what do you call the people at my event.

Asked more than once through the help widget, and it is a vocabulary question rather
than a how-to. The system uses several words for people and they are not interchangeable:

- **Delegate / attendee / registrant** — somebody who registers to come to the event and
  buys a ticket. All three words appear across the product and the guides for the same
  person. Everything about them is in
  [Delegate registration](/07-delegate-registration/) and, written for them rather than
  about them, [For attendees and exhibitors](/14-for-attendees-exhibitors/).
- **Submitter** — anybody with an Oxford Abstracts account who sends an abstract. Being a
  submitter says nothing about whether they are coming; those are separate lists.
- **Author / presenter** — named on a submission. An author need not be the submitter,
  and only the submitter can edit the submission.
- **Reviewer** — grades submissions assigned to them. Sometimes called referee, grader or
  judge.
- **Committee member** — decides outcomes.
- **Account administrator / event administrator** — see
  [[adding-and-removing-event-admins]].

**The one that costs support time:** submitters and delegates are separate populations
that happen to overlap. "Has this presenter registered?" means cross-referencing the
submissions table against the attendees table — there is no single view showing both, and
the old HubSpot article that covered it was never migrated.
