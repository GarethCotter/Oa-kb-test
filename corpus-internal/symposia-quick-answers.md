Transfer a symposium to a different organiser, change the symposium owner, symposium
email template, customise the submission URL, people can still attach abstracts to a
symposium, symposium reviewer link, interactive workshop form.

Distilled from support replies in the year to July 2026. Symposia is a separately
purchased add-on, so these come from a smaller group of organisers who ask a lot.

- **Changing who owns a symposium** is done by Oxford Abstracts support — give them
  the symposium number and the new owner's email address. Organisers cannot transfer
  ownership themselves.
- **Symposium emails have their own templates**, separate from the event's ordinary
  email templates. They live on the symposium emails page of the event dashboard, not
  under the main Emails section, which is why people report the templates "missing".
- **Submission URLs cannot be customised.** There is no vanity or branded submission
  link; the system's own URL is what you circulate.
- **People can still attach submissions to a symposium** when the attachments option
  is left open on the symposium dashboard. If submissions keep arriving after you
  meant to stop them, that setting is the cause — turn off allowing submissions to
  the symposium.
- **One reviewer link serves every reviewer.** You do not need a personalised link per
  person: the system decides which submissions to show from the email address they
  signed in with. This worries organisers sending a bulk email; it is correct.
