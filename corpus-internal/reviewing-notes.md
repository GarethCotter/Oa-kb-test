Adding reviewers in bulk fails, duplicate email addresses, paste a list of reviewers,
second stage reviewers cannot see first stage comments, can co-authors see the reviews,
who sees reviewer feedback, unassign incomplete reviewers.

- **Reviewers are not emailed automatically** when they are added to the system. You have
  to notify them yourself.
- **Preparing decisions during reviewing:** yes, you can start preparing decisions while
  reviewing is still ongoing.
- **Undoing a bulk decision:** reselect the submissions and choose a different decision
  type.
- **Auto-assign is all-or-nothing:** re-running it re-assigns every submission, not just
  some.

From a year of support replies, July 2026:

- **Adding a list of reviewers silently fails if the list contains a duplicate email
  address.** The whole import stops rather than skipping the repeat. Ask them to
  de-duplicate the list and try again — this is the first thing to check when a bulk
  add "just doesn't work", and organisers rarely spot it themselves.
- **Reviewers in a later stage cannot see reviews from an earlier one.** Reviewer data
  from previous stages is visible only to event admins, so a second-stage reviewer
  reading a full paper cannot see what the abstract reviewers said. If the committee
  needs that continuity, an admin has to carry it across.
- **Co-authors never see reviewer feedback**, and cannot see the abstract either. Only
  the submitter can, and only if the organiser has turned on showing reviews to
  submitters.
- **Unassigning everyone who did not complete their review** can be done in one go from
  the reviews table; reassigning to sweep-up reviewers afterwards is manual.
