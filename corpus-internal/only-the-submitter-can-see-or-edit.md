title: "Co-authors, presenters and panellists cannot see or edit the submission"
internal: true
last_reviewed: 2026-07-31
---

Co-author cannot see the abstract, presenter cannot upload their slides, my colleague
cannot find the submission, supervisor needs to edit, panellist wants to add a file,
nothing on my dashboard, transfer a submission to someone else.

Distilled from around 15 support tickets in the year to July 2026 — a rule that
surprises people every time.

**Only the submitter has the submission.** It appears on their dashboard and nobody
else's. Co-authors, presenters, panellists and supervisors named on an abstract cannot
see it, cannot edit it, and cannot upload files to it, even when they have their own
Oxford Abstracts account. A direct link to a submission only works for the person who
created it.

**When somebody else needs control**, an admin changes the owner of the submission
from the submissions table. That moves all correspondence to the new person too.

**Practical consequences worth saying out loud:**

- A panellist who wants to add their own slides must either send the file to the
  submitter, or make their own separate submission — which gives them their own
  submission ID.
- Co-authors only ever reach the system through the automatic email sent by **part 3**
  of the authors and affiliations question, which asks them their own extra questions.
  That link is the only thing they can open.
- Whether a co-author or presenter has registered for the event is visible to the
  organiser: open the submissions table, click **Show authors**, and read the paid
  status on the accordion rows.
- There is no automatic email asking co-authors to supply their details beyond that
  part 3 link; anything else has to be a manual email.
