---
title: "Inline comments versus comments to the author — why submitters see nothing"
internal: true
last_reviewed: 2026-07-31
---

Authors cannot see reviewer comments, inline comments not showing to submitters,
feedback missing, show reviews to submitters turned on but nothing appears, hide
comments from some submitters, inline comments missing from the Excel export.

**There are two separate places a reviewer can leave feedback**, and this is almost
always the answer when submitters say they can see nothing:

1. **"Comments to the author"** — a field on the review form.
2. **Inline comments** — notes attached to specific points in the text.

Turning on "inline comments visible to submitters" reveals only the second kind. If the
reviewers actually typed into "Comments to the author", the submitters will see nothing
even though the setting is on and everything looks correct. Check which one the
reviewers used before treating it as a fault — in every ticket of this shape so far,
the reviewers had used the other field.

**Showing comments is all or nothing.** You cannot reveal feedback to some submitters
and withhold it from others. The setting is on or off for everyone.

**Inline comments do not come out in the Excel export.** Only the general comments do.
Anyone building a spreadsheet of feedback needs to know that before they promise it to
a committee.
