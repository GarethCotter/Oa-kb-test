Standard questions missing from the list, guide lists seven but I see one, cannot add
abstract question, author approval not in the list, add question list is short.

Each standard question can appear **once per form**. The **Standard questions** group in
**+ Question** only offers the ones the form does not already have, so a form that already
uses most of them shows a very short list — often a single entry.

Nothing is broken and nothing is package-gated here. The questions that are not offered
are already on the form, further down the page.

Confirmed in the app on 7 August 2026: a demo form carrying Abstract, Author approval,
Author will attend, Permission to publish, Presentation and Poster presentation link
offered exactly one standard question, Program permission.

If someone wants a second question of the same kind — two abstract-style fields, say —
they build it from **Text questions → Formatted text**, which is the same editor without
the standard-question label.
