Covers: how much does it cost, what does it cost, pricing, price, plans, packages,
how much is the Professional Conference Package, is there a free version, how much
for symposia or certificates, what do I get for my money, cost per abstract, cost
per delegate, upgrading.

**The answer to any pricing question is the pricing page: https://oxfordabstracts.com/pricing**

It lists the current price of every plan and add-on. Give the reader that link and
tell them what they will find there. **Never quote a figure, a price band, or a
per-abstract cost** — those go stale, and a wrong number in a help answer is worse
than no number.

Do not send pricing questions to accounts@oxfordabstracts.com. That address is for
paying in an alternative currency, not for finding out what something costs.

The plans, cheapest first, are: **Basic** (free), **Abstract Management**,
**Standard Conference Package** and **Professional Conference Package**.
**Certificates**, **Symposia** and **Multi-stage** are bought separately, and
Certificates is included with Professional.

You also see the price for your own event when you create it, before you pay.
