Wrong name showing, change my email address, no role in this event, not registered,
no Oxford Abstracts account, email already exists, merge two accounts, delete my
account, signed in with the wrong address, Google sign-in, single sign-on, blank
dashboard.

Distilled from around 30 support tickets in the year to July 2026. Almost every
"the link doesn't work" ticket is one of these.

**An account must exist, under exactly the right address, before any link works.**
Reviewers, submitters and authors all need to have registered themselves. Assigning
someone submissions does not create an account for them. Until they register, the
reviewer list shows **Not registered**, the submissions table can show **No Oxford
Abstracts account**, and their name is blank — all of which look like faults and are
not. The name fills in the first time they log in.

**"You have no role in this event"** almost always means they are signed in with a
different address from the one on the submission or registration. People who use
Oxford Abstracts for several conferences routinely have two.

**Who can change what:**

- **Names and account email addresses** — only the account holder, from their own
  profile. An admin cannot do it, and neither can support. Name questions you add to
  the submission form are separate fields that an admin *can* edit, but changing them
  does not change the account name.
- **The owner of a submission** — an admin can move a submission to a different
  account from the submissions table. Leave the "send email" box ticked so the new
  owner gets their access link. This is the answer whenever the wrong person needs to
  edit something.
- **A reviewer's email address** — cannot be changed at all. Add the new address as a
  new reviewer and reassign their submissions.
- **Invoices and receipts** — only Oxford Abstracts can amend the name on those.

**Accounts created with Google or another single sign-on** cannot use the ordinary
password reset, and cannot change their email until SSO is removed or a password is
set from the profile page. Google users reset with Google.

**Merging two accounts is not possible** once both exist. The workaround is to move
the submissions to the preferred account, and for reviewing, to add the second address
as a reviewer and reassign.

**An account cannot be deleted while it still holds event content** — the organiser
removes the submission first. Users delete their own account from the profile menu;
support cannot do it for them.

**"Email is invalid" at sign-up** is usually a leading or trailing space in the
address.
