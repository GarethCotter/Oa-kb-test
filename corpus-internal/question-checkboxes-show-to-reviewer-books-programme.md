Reviewers only see the title, abstract book contains just titles, uploaded file not
showing in the programme, question missing from the book, reviewer cannot see the
abstract, content not appearing, show to reviewer, in books, in program.

The single most common configuration problem in the support mailbox, in several
disguises. Distilled from around 15 tickets in the year to July 2026.

**Every question on the submission form has its own checkboxes at the bottom of the
question editor**, and each controls one destination:

- **Show to reviewer** — whether reviewers see that answer. If reviewers report seeing
  only the title and category, this is switched off on the other questions. It is a
  per-question setting, not a limitation of the free package, and not a fault.
- **In abstract book** — whether the answer appears in the downloadable books. A book
  that comes out containing nothing but titles is this, on every other question.
- **In program** — whether the answer, including an uploaded file, appears in the online
  programme.
- **In poster gallery** — whether the answer appears in the poster gallery. Checked in the
  app on 7 August 2026: there are four destinations, not three. A poster or a set of
  keywords that is missing from the gallery while showing everywhere else is this one.

A question that takes no response, such as a text block, does not offer all four.

Open the question in **Submission form editor** and tick the ones you need. They are
independent of one another: a question can be visible to reviewers and absent from the
book, or the reverse.

**Two related traps in the same editor:**

- **Reviewers seeing "untitled submission" or bare ID numbers** means either "Show to
  reviewers" is off on the title question, or the wrong field has been set as the
  title question for that stage.
- **A confirmation email showing the whole abstract instead of the title** means the
  title setting has been applied to the wrong question. Move it to the actual title
  question.
