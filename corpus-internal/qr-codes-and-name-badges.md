---
title: "QR codes and barcodes on delegate name badges — one per event, not one per person"
internal: true
last_reviewed: 2026-07-31
---

QR code for each registrant, barcode per delegate, unique QR code per attendee,
scanning delegates in, name tag with a barcode, badge scanning, check-in by QR,
individual QR codes, can the system generate QR codes.

The most-asked limitation of the year: 17 support tickets in the twelve months to
July 2026, more than any other "can it do this" question.

**No — not per person.** Oxford Abstracts cannot generate an individual QR code or
barcode for each registrant to carry on their badge. What it produces is a single
common code for the whole programme, which anyone can scan to reach the event.

There is no workaround inside Oxford Abstracts, so say so plainly rather than
offering a half-answer. Organisers who need per-delegate scanning — for session
attendance, entry control or lead capture — do it with a separate badge-printing or
check-in service, feeding it an export of the attendee list from the registrations
table.

Do not confuse this with the name badge content itself, which is configurable in the
usual way; the limitation is specifically the per-person code.
