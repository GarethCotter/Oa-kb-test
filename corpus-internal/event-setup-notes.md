Book a support session, speak to someone, demo, phone number, set up a new event for
me, image dimensions, picture sizes, upgrade my plan.

- **Booking time with the team** is the answer to a surprising share of tickets — over
  40 in the year to July 2026 ended with it. Organisers can book a free one-to-one
  support session or a product demonstration through the online booking calendar at
  any time. There is **no telephone support line**; email or a booked session are the
  two routes.
- **Having Oxford Abstracts set up a new event** needs four things in the email: the
  organisation name, the event name, the event abbreviation (for example ABC2026), and
  the event notification email address, which must be one of the organiser's own.
- **Recommended image dimensions** for banners, logos and backgrounds are published in
  the help centre; using them avoids pixelated or cropped uploads.
- **Background image dimensions** for the event theme: 1920 x 600px.
- **Upgrading a plan:** direct people to the support/sales team rather than describing
  the steps.
- **Pricing questions:** see the dedicated pricing note — always oxfordabstracts.com/pricing,
  never a quoted figure.
- **Speaking to a human:** the contact form is the route; support replies as soon as they can.
