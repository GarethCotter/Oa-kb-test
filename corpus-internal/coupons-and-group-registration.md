---
title: "Coupon codes apply to the whole purchase, not to one person in a group booking"
internal: true
last_reviewed: 2026-07-31
---

Coupon code, discount code, promo code, group registration with a coupon, discount
applied to everyone, coupon used by the wrong person, adding a coupon after
registering, coupon not working.

**The behaviour that catches people out.** A coupon discounts the **whole purchase**,
not a named person within it. So when somebody registers a group and enters a code
meant for one of them, everyone on that booking gets the discount.

There is no per-person coupon. If it has already happened, the practical fix is to ask
the person who made the booking who the code was intended for, cancel the other
registrations on it, and have those people register separately.

**Adding a coupon to a registration that already exists** does not need support: open
the registrations table on the event dashboard, find the order, and apply the coupon
there.

**When a code "isn't working"** and it is not the group case above, it is worth asking
for the specific code, the registrant's email address, and a screenshot of the error
before escalating — support cannot diagnose it from the description alone, and that
round trip is most of the delay on these tickets.
