---
title: "Where do I pay Oxford Abstracts, or find my own invoice?"
audience: organisers
plan: all plans (including free Basic)
last_reviewed: 2026-08-03
---

Your own Oxford Abstracts bill — not your attendees' — is on the **Event payments**
page. Reach it from the **credit card icon in the top bar**, to the left of your
account initial, on any page inside the event. It lists each payment with its type,
the date created, the date paid, and a reference number, and carries a **Make
payment** link.

This is a different thing from the invoices your attendees receive for their
registration tickets, which live under **Registration**. Both are called "invoices",
which is why the question arrives ambiguously: someone asking where their invoice is
may mean either. If they mention paying Oxford Abstracts, a package, credit or an
overdue account, they mean this page.

The icon is easy to miss — it is unlabelled — so saying where it is, rather than
just naming the page, is usually the difference between an answered question and a
second ticket.
