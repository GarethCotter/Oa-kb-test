Password reset email not arriving, reset link not working, cannot log in, forgot my
password, reviewer cannot sign in, reset link expired, no reset email received,
unable to access my account, submission portal login.

Distilled from 22 support tickets in the year to July 2026 — the largest access
problem after reviewers not seeing their assignments.

**Check the site first — this is the commonest cause and nobody spots it.** There are
two Oxford Abstracts sites with logins, and people reset the wrong one:

- **app.oxfordabstracts.com** — the actual software: submitting, reviewing,
  registering, running an event. Reset here:
  **https://app.oxfordabstracts.com/auth/forgot-password**
- **help.oxfordabstracts.com** — this help centre. Its own reset page does nothing for
  the app, and someone who lands there will keep resetting a password that was never
  the problem.

If the reset link in an email came from the help centre, that is the fault.

**Then work down these:**

1. **The email is slow or filtered.** Ask them to check spam and wait a few minutes,
   then request it again from the app address above.
2. **They are trying a different address from the one on the account.** Someone using
   Oxford Abstracts for more than one conference often has two. Reviewers especially —
   the organiser assigns one address and the reviewer signs in with another.
3. **A stale session.** An incognito or private window rules this out in seconds and
   fixes a surprising number of cases.

**Support can reset a password manually** if none of that works. The person then
changes it themselves after signing in, from their profile page.

**One thing that is not the cause:** an organiser cannot reset a submitter's or
reviewer's password, and cannot change their email address. That has to come from the
account holder or from support.
