Exactly one author must be presenting, cannot submit presenting author, presenter error
on submission, how many presenters allowed, submitter stuck on authors question.

The authors and affiliations question has a setting for how many authors may be marked as
presenting. If a submitter marks a different number, they cannot complete the submission:
pressing **Submit** shows the authors question with a message such as *"Exactly one author
must be presenting - there are currently 0"*, and the submission stays incomplete.

Two things this is usually not: it is not the submitter missing a tick box elsewhere, and
it is not a fault. It is the organiser's own setting doing its job.

**To change it:** open the **Authors and affiliations** question on
**Abstract management → Submissions → Form & setup**. The number of presenters permitted
is the last field in the author fields section.

Worth knowing when a submitter is stuck: this rule is enforced at submit time, not while
they type, so they can fill the whole form before discovering it.
