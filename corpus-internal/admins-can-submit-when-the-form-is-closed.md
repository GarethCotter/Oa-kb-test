title: "The form works when I test it but submitters say it is closed"
internal: true
last_reviewed: 2026-08-07
---

Tested my own submission link and it worked, submitters say submissions are closed, form
looks open to me, preview works but link does not, admin can still submit.

Admins are not blocked by the form status. When submissions are closed, an event admin
opening the submission link still gets a working form, with a message saying submissions
are closed for submitters but that they can still create submissions as an admin.

So an organiser who tests their own link sees the form working, tells people it is fine,
and the people who actually matter are locked out. Confirmed in the app on 7 August 2026.

**What to tell them:** the status they need is in **Form status** on
**Abstract management → Submissions → Form & setup** — specifically **Open for new
submissions**. To test the way a submitter experiences it, open the link in a private
window signed out, or with an account that has no admin rights on that event.
