Send certificates to presenters only, certificate to some people not all, custom list
of recipients, certificate for presenters in the programme, who gets a certificate.

- **Sending to a chosen group rather than everyone:** the recipient options offer
  broad groups such as accepted presenters, which is not the same as "presenters who
  are actually in the programme". Use the **custom list** option instead, and build
  the address list by exporting from the submissions table with child rows included.
  This is the answer to almost every "I only want certificates to go to X" question.
- **Creating one:** from your Event Dashboard, scroll to the Certificate section in the
  left-hand column and click **Design**.
- **Adding certificates to your plan:** in the admin event portal, use the left-side
  navigation to find Certificates. Click into it and you'll see the option to upgrade
  your plan to include them.
- **Cost:** direct people to oxfordabstracts.com/pricing rather than quoting a figure.
