Reviewer cannot see assigned reviews, no submissions showing, reviewer dashboard
empty, "you have not been assigned to any reviews", reviewer shows as unregistered,
reviewer cannot access abstracts to review, reviewer sees IDs but no titles.

Distilled from 24 support replies to this exact problem. Work down the list — the
first two causes account for most of them.

**1. They are signed in with a different email address.** By far the most common
cause. The reviewer must sign in with the exact address the organiser assigned. A
reviewer who uses Oxford Abstracts for more than one conference often has two
addresses. Check the address shown top-right against the one in the By Reviewer
table.

**2. They have not registered yet.** The By Reviewer table shows **unregistered**
against their name until they do. Assigning submissions does not create an account —
they must register themselves, with that same address, at
app.oxfordabstracts.com. The name auto-fills once the account exists.

**3. Reviewing is not open for that stage.** Reviews only appear while the stage is
open. On a multi-stage event, check the specific stage rather than the event.

**4. The account is linked to Google or LinkedIn.** Unlinking it from the profile
page resolves it; see the profile article.

**5. An email alias.** An alias needs its own Oxford Abstracts account, and the
alias itself has to be on the reviewer list — it does not inherit from the main
address.

**6. The review form has no required question.** Reviews then auto-complete the
moment they are assigned, so there is nothing left to review. Make at least one
question required.

**7. Reviewers see ID numbers instead of titles.** The wrong question is set as the
title question on the submission form.

If none of that applies, support's usual last resort is to remove the reviewer from
the reviewer list, re-add them and reassign the submissions. Unassigning does not
delete review data — a completed review reappears as completed once the submission
is reassigned.

**Reviewers who contact Oxford Abstracts directly should be sent to their event
administrator** — the address is shown under the conference name on their dashboard.
Oxford Abstracts does not run the events and cannot change an event's settings.
