Download all the posters, bulk download uploaded files, get every presentation,
question response folders, accept PowerPoint uploads, PPT not allowed, collect
slides, download submitted files in one go.

- **Bulk downloading everything people uploaded**: **Abstract management → Reports and
  downloads**, under **Question response folders**. Organisers routinely think they
  have to open submissions one at a time; they do not.
- **Accepting PowerPoint as well as PDF**: use a **file upload** question type on the
  submission form rather than a poster upload question. The poster gallery itself
  takes single-page PDFs, but a file upload question will take slide decks.
- **File type and size limits** are per question type and are listed on the submission
  question types guide, under the multimedia questions section — worth quoting the
  actual limits rather than guessing, because they differ by question type.
