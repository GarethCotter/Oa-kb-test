Cancelling your registration or getting a refund

AttendingLast reviewed 29 July 2026

For conference attendeesOrganising the event?

Refunds are decided by the organisers of your conference, not by Oxford Abstracts. Here is who to ask, and what to expect.#

Who to ask#

Contact the organisers of your conference. Oxford Abstracts makes the software your conference runs on, but we are not involved in running the event itself. We cannot see your conference's refund policy, cannot decide whether you are entitled to one, and cannot refund you without the organisers asking us to.

The organisers set their own cancellation terms — often a deadline, sometimes a partial refund, sometimes none at all — and those terms are usually on the conference website or in the email confirming your registration.

You will find the organisers' email address under the conference name when you sign in to your dashboard at app.oxfordabstracts.com. See How do I contact the event administrator?

What to expect once a refund is agreed#

  • A refund cancels your place. Being refunded and being cancelled are the same thing, so you will no longer be registered for the conference.
  • It takes five to ten days to appear on your card statement once the organisers have processed it.
  • You will be refunded what you paid. Any fees charged to the organisers are their concern, not something taken off your refund.

If you were charged twice, or the amount looks wrong#

Tell the organisers, with the date and the amount. They can see every transaction for their event and can put it right. If something looks like a fault in the software rather than a mistake in your booking, they will raise it with us.

Your receipt or invoice#

If you need proof of payment for expenses, you can download it yourself — see Downloading invoices and receipts.
