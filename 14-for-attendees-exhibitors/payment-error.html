Receiving an error when trying to pay for your event with PayPal?#

If you are trying to pay for a ticket to attend an event but are receiving the following error message:

It could be due to PayPal not being available in your country.

Please see the country list where PayPal is available.

If your country is not on the list, then please contact your event administrator so they can provide another payment solution.
