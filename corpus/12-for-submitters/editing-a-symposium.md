# Editing a Symposium

##

NB: The guidance below is for submitters of a symposium. If you are the administrator of an event please see [Symposia.](/09-add-ons/)

[Skip to written instructions](#TEX)

To edit a symposium you have already created, log into [https://app.oxfordabstracts.com](https://app.oxfordabstracts.com/)  and click on the pencil icon, then **Amend symposium** on your dashboard. You can edit it, even if it is complete, subject to deadlines.

![](/assets/img/s3-amazonaws-comcdn-freshdesk-comdatahelpdeskattachmentsprod-757a20a6.webp)  
  

You can then amend any of the fields...

![](/assets/img/s3-amazonaws-comcdn-freshdesk-comdatahelpdeskattachmentsprod-8f0bd1c9.webp)

Make the changes required then click on **Submit**.

  
  

After pressing **Submit,**you then have to option to add, (blue) re-order (by dragging and dropping - red), or remove abstract submissions (green).

![](/assets/img/s3-amazonaws-comcdn-freshdesk-comdatahelpdeskattachmentsprod-7d70be65.webp)  

If the symposium submission settings allow for it, you can also invite users to attach submissions.

![](/assets/img/s3-amazonaws-comcdn-freshdesk-comdatahelpdeskattachmentsprod-f15d8522.webp)  
  

At the bottom of the page, there is the option to submit an abstract (this will be subject to settings and deadlines).

Click **Continue** to return to your personal dashboard.

![](/assets/img/s3-amazonaws-comcdn-freshdesk-comdatahelpdeskattachmentsprod-4a2749ad.webp)
