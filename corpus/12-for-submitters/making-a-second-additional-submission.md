# Making a second/additional submission

## If the event settings allow, you can make additional submissions to the same event, if you require.

NB: The guidance below is for users who are submitting to a conference. If you are the administrator of an event please see [The submission stage](/02-submissions/).

[Skip to written instructions.](#TEX)

To make a new submission to a conference you've already submitted to, click on **New Submission** in your personal dashboard.

  
  
![](/assets/img/undefined-feb-22-2021-11-39-15-35-am-6f4fce7b.webp)  

To make a submission to a new conference that you haven't yet submitted to, follow the link in the conference website or email or contact the Event Administrator.

  

See also [Making a submission](/12-for-submitters/making-a-submission) and [Editing a submission](/12-for-submitters/editing-an-abstract-or-submission).![](/assets/img/oxfordabstracts-freshdesk-comsupportsolutionsarticles8000072-918586b6.gif)
