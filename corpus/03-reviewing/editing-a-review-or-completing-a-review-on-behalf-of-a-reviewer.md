# Editing a review or completing a review on behalf of a reviewer

## You can edit or complete a review on behalf of an assigned reviewer, should you require.

The guidance below is for event administrators/ organisers. If you are an end user (eg. submitter, reviewer, delegate etc), please click [here](https://oxfordabstracts.com/resources/contact-support/).

NB: in order to complete a review on behalf of someone else, the submission must be [assigned to a reviewer](/03-reviewing/assigning-and-unassigning-a-submission-to-a-reviewer.html).

Go to **[Event dashboard](/01-getting-started/event-dashboard.html) → Reviews**

Skip to [**View as a reviewer**](#VAR)

There are two tables -  the default option is**Reviews by submission** (ordered according to submission ID).  You can edit or complete reviews here. If you wish to view the review as a reviewer would, you will need to go to **Reviews by reviewer** (see below)

It's helpful to be familiar with [The Reviews Table](/03-reviewing/the-review-tables.html) before you begin editing or completing a review.

Click anywhere in the row of the submission that you wish to edit.![](/assets/img/undefined-feb-24-2021-11-10-46-84-am-8ac6c8dd.webp)  
  
  

You will see two tabs at the top of the screen - **Submission**, and **Reviews.** The Submission will display by default in this view.

Click on the 1) **Reviews** tab. You can access other reviews of any submission in this view, by 2) clicking on the relevant email address in the left hand part of the panel.

![](/assets/img/undefined-feb-24-2021-04-21-56-44-pm-3a94a6b2.webp)  

**3)** Click  **Next** in the top right hand of the screen to access the reviews for the next submission.

  
  

To edit the response of any question, click on **Edit Response**

![](/assets/img/s3-amazonaws-comcdn-freshdesk-comdatahelpdeskattachmentsprod-fedbd06c.webp)   
  

Make your changes and click **Done**to save.

  
![](/assets/img/s3-amazonaws-comcdn-freshdesk-comdatahelpdeskattachmentsprod-dea89380.webp) 

#### 

#### **View a review as a reviewer**

  

Click in your chosen row as above

Click **View as reviewer** in the **Reviews by reviewer** table.

![](/assets/img/s3-amazonaws-comcdn-freshdesk-comdatahelpdeskattachmentsprod-f0f5f2f6.webp)   
  

Click **Start reviewing** to complete or view the review. When you have finished, click **Close reviewer view.**
