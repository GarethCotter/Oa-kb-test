# Symposia reviewing

Reviewing symposia works much like [reviewing abstracts](/03-reviewing/opening-and-closing-reviewing), with one difference worth knowing up front: you cannot assign symposia by category.

This guide is for event administrators, and follows the order you will need it: assign, open, notify, then step in where a review needs finishing.

## 1. Assign reviewers

Add your reviewers to the system first, via [Manage users](/01-getting-started/manage-users).

You can then assign symposia to reviewers, or reviewers to symposia, in the same way as [assigning abstract reviews](/03-reviewing/assigning-and-unassigning-a-submission-to-a-reviewer) — go to **Event dashboard → **Symposium → Reviews**.

## 2. Open reviewing

Go to **Event dashboard → **Symposium → Dashboard** and scroll to the **Reviews** section. On the right you will see **Review status** with an open/close toggle beneath it.

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2024-03-04-at-15-39-59-png-be58278f.webp)

Click the toggle to open reviewing. It turns peach when open and grey when closed.

## 3. Notify your reviewers

Go to **Event dashboard → **Emails → Edit & Send → Symposium**. Scroll to **Manually sent emails** and, under the symposium review emails, click **Notify symposium reviewers**.

![](/assets/img/undefined-may-26-2021-08-03-39-90-am-046b9e75.webp)

Check the message and make any changes you need — they save automatically. For more on editing templates, see [Amending template emails](/05-emails/editing-the-template-emails).

When you are ready, click **Sending**.

![](/assets/img/s3-amazonaws-comcdn-freshdesk-comdatahelpdeskattachmentsprod-ad26b35b.webp)

Tick individual recipients to send in batches, or tick the top box to select everyone, then click **Send emails**.

![](/assets/img/s3-amazonaws-comcdn-freshdesk-comdatahelpdeskattachmentsprod-351c198e.webp)

## 4. Edit a review, or complete one on behalf of a reviewer

You can edit, complete or submit a review for an assigned reviewer — useful when someone drops out late.

Go to **Event dashboard → **Symposium → Reviews → By symposium** or **By reviewer**.

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2024-04-17-at-12-24-37-png-53c88d0c.webp)

Rows with a pink background are incomplete. Click anywhere in the row you want.

![](/assets/img/undefined-may-26-2021-08-34-21-99-am-f06d9ab5.webp)

Three tabs appear at the top — **Symposium**, **Attach submission** and **Reviews**. The symposium shows by default, so click **Reviews**.

![](/assets/img/s3-amazonaws-comcdn-freshdesk-comdatahelpdeskattachmentsprod-d014a40b.webp)

Click **Edit response** on any question to change it.

![](/assets/img/undefined-may-26-2021-08-36-09-59-am-706aeb03.webp)

Click **Done** when you have finished.

![](/assets/img/undefined-may-26-2021-08-36-51-59-am-5739da85.webp)
