# Symposia decisions & emails

The last stage: recording outcomes for your symposia and telling people about them. Both work like their abstract equivalents, with templates written for symposia.

This guide is for event administrators.

## Recording decisions

Go to **Event dashboard → **Symposium → Decisions**.

Decisions are made in the symposium decisions table exactly as they are in the submissions decisions table, so follow [Recording a decision](/04-decisions/recording-decisions-accept-reject-or-withdraw).

To withdraw a symposium rather than reject it, see [Managing symposia](/09-add-ons/managing-symposia).

## Symposia emails

Go to **Event dashboard → **Emails → Edit & Send → Symposium**.

The email tool works as it does elsewhere, so it is worth being familiar with [creating](/05-emails/creating-custom-emails) and [sending](/05-emails/sending-and-scheduling-emails) emails first.

### Automatic emails

These send themselves when something happens. There are six, triggered when:

1. A symposium submitter logs in and amends an existing symposium.
2. A submitter invites a registered user to submit an abstract to their symposium.
3. A submitter invites an unregistered user to do the same.
4. A submission is attached to their symposium.
5. They create a symposium.
6. A symposium is submitted on their behalf.

### Manually sent emails

These you send yourself, when you are ready.

To symposium submitters:

- those whose submissions are incomplete
- all submitters, regardless of status
- a free template for any other announcement
- those whose symposia have been accepted
- those whose symposia have been rejected

![](/assets/img/undefined-jan-31-2022-11-08-10-41-am-13d61f69.webp)

To reviewers:

- notify them that reviewing is open
- remind them of the reviewing deadline
- a free template for a custom email

![](/assets/img/undefined-feb-07-2022-11-29-38-31-am-40381e78.webp)

For how to send them in batches, see [Symposia reviewing](/09-add-ons/symposia-reviewing).
