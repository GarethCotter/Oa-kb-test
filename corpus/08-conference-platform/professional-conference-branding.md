# Professional Conference - branding

## This feature is only available in the Professional Conference package.

The guidance below is for event administrators/ organisers. If you are an end user (eg. submitter, reviewer, delegate etc), please click [here](https://oxfordabstracts.com/resources/contact-support/).

Go to

**Event dashboard ➞ Conference ➞ Program ➞ Builder**

then

**Display  ➞ Formatting  ➞ Branding**

**![](/assets/img/undefined-feb-02-2022-04-23-09-11-pm-98e6dd8a.webp)**

The **Program branding** panel will open. At the top you will see a preview of the part of the program you will be formatting. To the right of each section, you'll see a + icon. Click to display the options.

(Note - 6) Sponsorship guidance is [here](/08-conference-platform/professional-conference-sponsors).)

![](/assets/img/undefined-feb-02-2022-04-26-21-45-pm-1d95d90f.webp)

1) Title

Click the dropdown to see the following. The first two options will be pulled from your [event details.](/01-getting-started/event-details)

If you would prefer, you can enter your own choice of title.

![](/assets/img/undefined-aug-11-2021-02-40-34-27-pm-fdad26bb.webp)

 2) The background image displays behind the program.

![](/assets/img/undefined-aug-11-2021-02-53-15-32-pm-ed9016a2.webp)

3) The top image will display at the top of the program and aligned to the centre.  A letterbox image is preferable here.

![](/assets/img/undefined-aug-11-2021-02-49-06-79-pm-00213acb.webp)

4)  You can choose to use the event colours that are set in [Event theme](/01-getting-started/event-theme), or choose custom colours for the program. If you pick **Choose program colours**, the options below will be displayed. There are three options - **Primary**, **Secondary** and **Background**. Take a look at the visual (top of screen) to see where these will be displayed.

![](/assets/img/undefined-feb-02-2022-04-29-29-25-pm-973e93f2.webp)

To set your colours, click in each colour bar.

![](/assets/img/undefined-aug-23-2021-10-05-31-14-am-6e3d379c.webp)

 1) Move the cursor round to pick your colour

 2) Use the pipette to hover over a colour to pick

![](/assets/img/undefined-aug-11-2021-03-00-17-67-pm-5e2dcf99.webp)3) Current colour

4) Move the cursor to the left to change the colour range in 1)

5) Enter RGB codes.

Ensure you click **Save** to retain your choices.
