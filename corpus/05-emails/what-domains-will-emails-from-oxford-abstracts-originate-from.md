# What domains will emails from Oxford Abstracts originate from?

## The below article will provide you with details of what domain Oxford Abstracts emails will originate from.

Emails will originate from the oxfordabstacts.com domain unless you choose to register a verified sender email address.

  

The emails will originate from the following:

  

[info@oxfordabstracts.com](mailto:info@oxfordabstracts.com)

[support@oxfordabstracts.com](mailto:support@oxfordabstracts.com)

[no-reply@oxfordabstracts.com](mailto:no-reply@oxfordabstracts.com)

  
  

Verified emails will appear to recipients as coming from ‘[example@email.com](mailto:example@email.com) via pm.mtasv.net;.

  

If you require emails to come directly from your chosen domain without a ‘via’ address, you will need to add a DKIM TXT record to your email DNS.

  

***Do the domains in question have applicable SPF and DKIM records in place to send authenticated emails?***

  

Yes
