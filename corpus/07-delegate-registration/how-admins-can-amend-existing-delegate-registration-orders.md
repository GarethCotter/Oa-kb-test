# How admins can amend existing delegate registration orders

## In the below article, you’ll find out how admins can amend existing orders on the attendee table of delegate registration.

Please Note: This article is for Administrators only!

You can make the following amendments without changing the order number:

* Change Tickets
* Add/remove Tickets
* Add/remove Add-ons
* Request Payment
* Issue a Refund

Skip to:

* [Change Tickets / Add or Remove Tickets & Add-ons](#make-amends)
* [Issue Refund](#refunding-attendee)
* [Request Payment](#request-payment)

**How to Make an Amendment**

From your dashboard, click on **Registration** on the **left-hand column** and **then click Registrations.**

**![](/assets/img/screenshot-2024-03-20-at-15-11-15-png-1-65b130b3.webp)**

Next, find the attendee you want to change the order of by using the **search bar** at the top of the table or scrolling through the table.

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2023-06-20-at-17-11-38-png-7610dec0.webp)

When found, **click on the row of the attendee order**.

**![](/assets/img/screenshot-2023-06-20-at-17-11-57-png-abfcae1a.webp)**

A panel will appear to your right.

Click **the three dots** at the top right of this panel and select **Edit or refund order**. The same option covers both amending an order and refunding one.

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2023-06-20-at-17-12-17-png-dbb010ae.webp)

You will now see what tickets belong to the ticket holder.

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2023-06-20-at-17-12-37-png-7240c09c.webp)

You can either “**remove all**” tickets the holder currently has, or you can **individually edit** whichever ticket or add-on you want to remove by **clicking on the X** next to the ticket.

**![](/assets/img/screenshot-2023-06-20-at-17-13-01-png-1e3c0d7e.webp)**

You can add Tickets by **clicking the +Ticket button** and selecting which ticket needs to be added from the drop-down menu.

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2023-06-20-at-17-13-20-png-8fd96317.webp)

If you need to add an “add-on” to a specific ticket, **select the +Add-On button** under the correlating ticket and select from the drop-down menu.

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2023-06-20-at-17-13-42-png-1b961428.webp)

When you make these changes, a screen to your right will appear, showing you the changes you are making and whether a refund or payment is required.

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2023-06-20-at-17-15-09-png-93a96faf.webp)

When you have completed your edits, click the **Continue Button** at the bottom left of the page.

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2023-06-20-at-17-15-31-png-0f1eefc5.webp)

Next, you will either need to refund an attendee or [request further payment](#request-payment).

**Refunding an Attendee**

At the moment, you can only process refunds offline e.g. such as a bank transfer. When this is complete, you can manually update the table.

You will also be able to preview the email that will be sent to the attendee regarding any changes to their order.

When you have checked this, you can then click the **Submit Button** at the bottom left of the screen.

**![](/assets/img/screenshot-2023-06-20-at-17-16-04-png-e1c9f675.webp)**

On the next screen, the status at the top of the side panel will have changed to **Pending Refund**, and on the main table under the payment column, you will see it says **Amendment.**

**![](/assets/img/screenshot-2023-06-20-at-17-16-24-png-8a32f2c5.webp)**

When you have issued the refund offline, you will go back into the order (complete the steps at the beginning of this article to find the correct attendee) and select **Mark edit refund as complete button** on the right-hand panel.

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2023-06-20-at-17-16-48-png-0846dbe2.webp)

You will then see the status has been updated to Paid on the right-hand panel and on the main attendee table.

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2023-06-20-at-17-17-05-png-9e8b2cd6.webp)

The attendee will receive another email to say they have been issued a refund.

**Making an amendment and requesting payment**

If you have made an amendment for an attendee and they now owe you money, on the screen, you can review your changes and preview the email that will be sent to the attendee.

When happy, click on the **Confirm and Send Request Button** at the bottom left of the page.

**![](/assets/img/screenshot-2023-06-20-at-17-17-45-png-cde10586.webp)**

While waiting to receive payment, the panel will show “Pending Payment”.

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2023-06-20-at-17-18-02-png-2dcd902e.webp)

The attendee can either click the payment link in the email they have received or log into their dashboard to follow the payment instructions and pay the outstanding balance.

When the balance has been paid, on the attendee table, you will see under the payment column that it will now say “paid”.

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2023-06-20-at-17-18-22-png-f1fb293d.webp)
