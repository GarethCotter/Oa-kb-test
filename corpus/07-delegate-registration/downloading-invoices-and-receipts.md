# Downloading invoices and receipts

## Learn how attendees can download their invoices or receipts when they have purchased a ticket through delegate registration.

Alternatively you can read and follow the step by step instructions [here.](#instructions)

When an attendee is in the process of buying their tickets, when payment is completed, on the Order Details Confirmation screen they will see a dropdown box at the top right of the page called Download Receipt.

  

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2023-10-02-at-14-48-03-png-f95c8ae2.webp)

  

Click on this to download the receipt or invoice.

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2023-10-02-at-14-48-09-png-5d421a53.webp)

## Sending it to them yourself

Most of the time it is quicker to send it than to talk someone through finding it.

Go to **Event dashboard → Registration → Registrations table**, click the
attendee's row, and use:

* **Email** to send them the **Receipt** or the **Invoice** directly
* **Order downloads** to save a copy for yourself

Please note: an order is recorded against the **buyer**, who may not be the person
attending. If you cannot find someone in the attendee list, they may have had a
ticket bought for them. See [Buyers, ticket holders and who receives what](/07-delegate-registration/buyers-ticket-holders-and-who-receives-what.html).

NB: this page is about the receipt and invoice **documents**. If you want to let
delegates **pay** by invoice or bank transfer instead of by card, see
[Letting attendees pay by invoice or bank transfer](/07-delegate-registration/letting-attendees-pay-by-invoice-or-bank-transfer.html).

## How an attendee gets it themselves

If an attendee needs to download it later, they can access it via their dashboard.

  

All they need to do is log into their Oxford Abstracts account with their attendee email address (the email they used to set up their attendee account), go to the box of the event they wish to obtain their invoice/receipt from, click on View Details.

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2023-10-02-at-14-48-20-png-9be335b0.webp)  
  

On the next screen click on the dropdown box at the top right of the page called Download Receipt. Then choose to download the receipt or invoice.

  

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2023-10-02-at-14-48-27-png-636b69c2.webp)
