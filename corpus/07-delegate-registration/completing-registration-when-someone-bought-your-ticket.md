# Completing registration when someone bought your ticket

## Learn how to complete you form when attendee and the ticket has been bought on your behalf.

This information is for attendees only.

Firstly, you will have received an email to your email address the ticket is registered to.

Open the email and click on the **Complete Registration button**.

  

Or alternatively **login** into your **Oxford Abstracts dashboard** where you can see your ticket.

On the ticket it will say **Incomplete**, **click on the Complete Form button**.

  

Either way will take you directly to the registration form for you to complete.

When completed click the **Save button** at the bottom of the page.
