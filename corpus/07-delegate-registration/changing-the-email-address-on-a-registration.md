# Changing the email address on a registration

## Here you can find instructions on how to change a registration email address for delegate registration.

Please note this information is for Admins ONLY!

From your **Event Dashboard,** on the l**eft-hand column**, click on **Registration** **→ Tickets → Registrations.**

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2024-03-21-at-12-21-20-png-6dad20c9.webp)  
  

**Search for the attendee** you want to change the registration email address in the s**earch box** at the top left of the table and then  **click on their name.**

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2023-08-25-at-11-08-28-png-cc35a2b5.webp)  
  

On the column that appears to the right of the screen, under F**orm Details, click on the arrow symbol** under the **email tab**.

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2023-08-25-at-11-08-35-png-f4234e43.webp)

  

**Add** the **new email** address to the **New Email tab.**

  

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2023-08-25-at-11-08-48-png-e632d311.webp)

  

If you want to **notify both attendees of the change,** make sure the **Send Email Notifications box is ticked** and **click on the eye symbol** to **view the emails** that will be **sent to each attendee.**

  

If you **do not want to send an email,** then please **untick this box.**

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2023-08-25-at-11-09-07-png-13df6f36.webp)  
  

Then **click the Assign button** to make the changes.

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2023-08-25-at-11-09-13-png-6631cffe.webp)
