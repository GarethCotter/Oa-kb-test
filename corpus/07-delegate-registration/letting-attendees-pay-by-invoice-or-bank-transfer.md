# Letting attendees pay by invoice or bank transfer

## Set up offline payments so delegates whose institution cannot pay by card can request an invoice and pay you directly.

The guidance below is for event administrators/ organisers. If you are an end user (eg. submitter, reviewer, delegate etc), please click [here](https://oxfordabstracts.com/resources/contact-support/).

Many university, hospital and government delegates cannot pay by card at all —
their finance department pays against an invoice. Oxford Abstracts calls this
**offline payments**, and it sits under **delayed payments** alongside payment
links.

The attendee chooses to pay by invoice at checkout, you receive the order, and
they transfer the money to you directly.

## Before you start: the fee collection card

Offline payments will not switch on until you have added a **fee collection
card**. Because the delegate pays you directly rather than through Stripe or
PayPal, Oxford Abstracts has no way to take its fee from the transaction, so it
needs a card on file to collect against instead.

NB: this card is for our fees only. Your delegates' money goes to you.

## Setting it up

Go to **Event dashboard → Registration → Finance → Payment providers**.

You will see **Invoice payments** in the list of providers, marked *Not
connected*, with a note that it requires a fee collection card.

To add the card and configure the rest, click **Configure invoice and receipts**
at the top right, then open the **Delayed payments** tab. Click **Add fee
collection card** and follow the prompts.

## Putting your bank details on the invoice

This is the step people miss, and without it your delegates receive an invoice
with no way to pay it.

On the **Delayed payments** tab, use the payment details section to record how you
want to be paid — your bank name, account name, IBAN or account number, sort code
or SWIFT, and any reference you want quoted. This is what appears on the invoice
the delegate receives.

## Asking for a PO number, VAT number or company address

Institutional finance departments usually need specific details on the invoice
before they will pay it — a purchase order number, a VAT or tax number, or the
billing address of the department rather than the delegate.

On the same **Delayed payments** tab, under **Custom questions for delayed
payments**, click **Add question**. Anything you ask here is put to attendees who
choose to pay by invoice, and **their answers appear on the invoice**.

Tip: add a question for each thing your delegates' finance teams routinely ask
for. A purchase order number and a company address cover most cases, and
collecting them up front saves reissuing invoices later.

## What your delegates see

At checkout, delegates choosing to pay by invoice answer your custom questions,
complete their order, and receive an invoice showing your payment details and
their answers. The order appears in your **Registrations table** straight away,
so you can see who has registered before the money arrives.

Please note: an order paid by invoice is recorded against the **buyer**, who may
not be the person attending. See [Buyers, ticket holders and who receives what](/07-delegate-registration/buyers-ticket-holders-and-who-receives-what.html).

## Related settings worth knowing

**Payment links** are the other kind of delayed payment, configured on the same
tab.

**Authorize.net** is a separate provider and has its own restriction: it can only
be used if your event currency is USD.

Remember: you can have card payments and invoice payments switched on at the same
time. Most organisers do, so that delegates who can pay by card do, and only those
who cannot use the slower route.

If you need help setting this up, [contact our Support Team](https://oxfordabstracts.com/resources/contact-support/).
