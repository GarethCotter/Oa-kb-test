# Running a sequential Multi-stage event

## Multi -stage abstract submission can be used for parallel stages (eg. Clinical and Practice) or sequential. This article provides guidance on how to manage the sequential type of multi-stage submission.

The guidance below is for event administrators/ organisers. If you are an end user (eg. submitter, reviewer, delegate etc), please click [here](https://help.oxfordabstracts.com/knowledge/support?hsLang=en).

To add [Multi-stage](https://oxfordabstracts.com/product/multi-stage-submission/) to your event, contact sales@oxfordabstracts.com.

1. Set up your [stages](/02-submissions/setting-up-a-multi-stage-event) and submission deadlines in the [event details.](/01-getting-started/event-details)
2. Set up your [submission](/02-submissions/creating-multi-stage-submission-forms) and [review forms.](/02-submissions/multi-stage-reviews)
3. [Open the call for abstracts](/02-submissions/opening-and-closing-submissions-deadlines) on **Stage 1**.
4. When you are finished, go through the [review process](https://help.oxfordabstracts.com/knowledge/the-reviewing-stage?hsLang=en) for **Stage 1**.
5. [Make decisions](/02-submissions/multi-stage-the-decision-stage) on **Stage 1**, and move the successful submissions to **Stage 2**.
6. [Notify all rejected submitters](/04-decisions/notifying-submitters-of-their-outcomes) from **Stage 1**.  Those that have had their submissions moved to **Stage 2** should be [notified from Stage 2](/04-decisions/notifying-submitters-of-their-outcomes) and be asked to [log in to their account](/01-getting-started/creating-an-account-with-oxford-abstracts-and-logging-in), where they will see their submissions, now in the second stage.
7. **Stage 2** submitters can then [amend their submission form](/12-for-submitters/editing-an-abstract-or-submission) by adding their full paper, and answer any additional questions. Ensure that you have set the questions that have already been answered in **Stage 1**, [to hide or read-only](/02-submissions/question-setting-tags) if you do not want submitters to edit any of the prior responses.
8. Set **Stage 2** status to the following, to disallow any new submissions (the bottom two as per your requirements).  
   ![](/assets/img/screenshot-2025-08-01-at-11-07-49-png-2c28a66b.webp)
9. When submissions are collected, you can then go through the [reviewing](https://help.oxfordabstracts.com/knowledge/the-reviewing-stage?hsLang=en) and [decision](https://help.oxfordabstracts.com/knowledge/the-decision-stage?hsLang=en) process on Stage 2.


## Common questions


### I am running an event where abstracts are submitted, reviewed and decided upon.  After that the submitters of accepted abstracts are asked to submit their full paper. How can I set this up?

[Multi-stage](https://help.oxfordabstracts.com/knowledge/multi-stage?hsLang=en) is ideal for this scenario or where a second stage review is required and you wish to retain not overtype the first stage submission or review data.
