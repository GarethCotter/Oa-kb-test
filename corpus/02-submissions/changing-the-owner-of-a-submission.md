# Changing the owner of a submission

## Learn how to change the submission owner by following the instructions below

This article is for Admins ONLY.

**Please note:** You cannot add a new submitters name.

This can only be added by the user when they register with Oxford Abstracts.

From your **Event Dashboard,** click on **Abstract Management, then Submissions and Table.**

**![](/assets/img/screenshot-2025-08-13-at-14-48-59-png-6fdb6a70.webp)**

From here **tick** the **submission/s** you wish to change the owner of by **selecting the box on the left-hand side.**

**![](/assets/img/screenshot-2022-10-06-at-16-17-34-png-17b1cbad.webp)**

Next click on the **Cog icon** above the submission table, and then **select Change Submission Owner.**

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2022-10-06-at-15-52-05-png-c8294e2f.webp)

On the next screen, you’ll see a box with the current submitter's email address.

The box next to it allows you to add the new submitter's email address.

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2022-10-06-at-15-52-11-png-5c08eddf.webp)

**Add** the **new email address** and click on **Assign**.

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2022-10-06-at-15-52-16-png-86401006.webp)

You can tick/untick the box underneath to allow an email to be sent to both email addresses updating them on this change.

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2022-10-06-at-15-52-22-png-ec7d37c9.webp)

You are also able to **view the Change Owner Log** to which submissions have change owners and who is responsible for this action.

Click the **Cog icon** above the table and **click** on the **Owner Change Log.**

**![](/assets/img/screenshot-2022-10-06-at-15-52-27-png-a5e58a26.webp)**

From here you can view all submissions that have changed owners.

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2022-10-06-at-15-52-31-png-70d22149.webp)

Should you require any further assistance, please contact our support desk via our [Contact Form.](https://oxfordabstracts.com/resources/contact-support/)
