# Deleting, restoring or withdrawing a submission

## An account or event administrator can delete or withdraw any unwanted submissions.

The guidance below is for event administrators/ organisers. If you are an end user (eg. submitter, reviewer, delegate etc), please click [here](https://help.oxfordabstracts.com/knowledge/support?hsLang=en).

[Skip to text instructions.](#TEX)

Go to **[Event dashboard](//help.oxfordabstracts.com/knowledge/event-dashboard?hsLang=en) → Abstract Management → Submissions → Table**

Skip to:

[Deleting a submission](#DEL)

[Restoring deleted submissions](#RES)

[Withdrawing a submission](#WIT)

#### Deleting a submission

In the [**Submissions table**](//help.oxfordabstracts.com/knowledge/the-submissions-table?hsLang=en), select the submissions you wish to delete by ticking the checkbox(es) on the left of each submission ID![](/assets/img/undefined-feb-24-2021-09-45-25-38-am-3dfe358b.webp)

  
  

Once selected, click on the **Delete Selected** button in the top right hand corner of the table.

![](/assets/img/s3-amazonaws-comcdn-freshdesk-comdatahelpdeskattachmentsprod-03179987.webp)  

The deleted submissions can be viewed by clicking on **View deleted**

![](/assets/img/undefined-feb-24-2021-09-46-34-29-am-2846f59e.webp)  
  

The **Deleted Submissions** table will display all submissions which have been deleted.

  
![](/assets/img/s3-amazonaws-comcdn-freshdesk-comdatahelpdeskattachmentsprod-f619a139.webp)  

---

**Restoring deleted submissions**

  

To restore a deleted submission, in the **Deleted Submissions table**, select the submissions you wish to restore by ticking the checkbox on the left of each submission ID and click **Restore Selected**

![](/assets/img/undefined-feb-24-2021-09-47-49-61-am-19ce7a00.webp)  
  

The restored submissions will now be restored to the main submission table.

#### 

#### **Withdrawing a submission**

  

To withdraw a submission, go to [**Event dashboard**](//help.oxfordabstracts.com/knowledge/event-dashboard?hsLang=en) → **Decisions** →

Ensure the **Decision** column is visible (in the **Decisions responses** section\_.

![](/assets/img/s3-amazonaws-comcdn-freshdesk-comdatahelpdeskattachmentsprod-f67dfefe.webp)  

Navigate to the **Decision** column in the table. Click on the dropdown menu in the relevant row and select **Withdrawn**.

NB: Withdrawing a submission isn't the same as deleting, as it will still remain in the submission and review tables.

![](/assets/img/s3-amazonaws-comcdn-freshdesk-comdatahelpdeskattachmentsprod-3a4b1556.webp)


## Common questions


### How do I withdraw or delete a submission as an administrator?

To withdraw (as an administrator), see [Recording a decision (Accepting/ rejecting/ withdrawing a submission)](/04-decisions/recording-decisions-accept-reject-or-withdraw). Alternatively, if you wish to delete a submission, see [Deleting/ restoring or withdrawing a submission](/02-submissions/deleting-restoring-or-withdrawing-a-submission).

If you are a submitter, contact the event administrator. You will see the email in the relevant event on your dashboard.
