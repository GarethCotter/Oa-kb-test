# Adding chair's email addresses

## Find out how to add chair email addresses for your event.

NB: This article is for ADMINS ONLY.

[Skip to adding multiple chair email address in one go.](#adding-multiple-chair-emails)

### **How to manually add chair email addresses, individually.**

From your **Dashboard → Conference → Program → Builder**

Next click on **Settings** towards the top right of the page.

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2022-06-30-at-15-57-33-png-726be949.webp)

Next click on **Chairs** from the drop-down menu.

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2022-06-30-at-15-58-00-png-cff84bbf.webp)

Then select **Fields**.

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2022-06-30-at-15-58-24-png-2c7e36a0.webp)

Click on the **“eye” symbol** next to **Email** and **select Required.**

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2022-06-30-at-16-12-54-png-0ad0dda9.webp)

**Select the Chairs tab** to **add** the **name of the chair** and their **email address**.

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2022-06-30-at-15-59-30-png-5030073f.webp)

To add another Chairs details, click **+ ADD ANOTHER CHAIRS.**

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2022-06-30-at-16-00-01-png-eded7f0a.webp)

### Adding multiple chairs at the same time

From your **Dashboard → Conference → Program → Builder**

Next click on **Settings** towards the top right of the page.

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2022-06-30-at-16-01-22-png-1fc0f240.webp)

Next click on **Chairs** from the drop-down menu.

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2022-06-30-at-16-02-25-png-70c4a161.webp)

Select **Add Multiple.**

**![](/assets/img/screenshot-2022-06-30-at-16-02-48-png-306d4bdb.webp)**

Whatever fields have been selected in the fields tab, this is what you will complete in this section (see bolded words on the image below)

Don’t forget to **add the commas** after each field you input e.g. *Mr, Joe, Jones,* (see below image).

When finished, click **Save** and return to the **Chair tab** where you will see all Chairs that you have added.

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2022-06-30-at-16-03-16-png-396d26ee.webp).
