# Abstract management checklist

## It is recommended that administrators go through the following checklist to set up the event

### 

**Event details**

Click [Event details](/01-getting-started/event-details) in the [Event Dashboard](/01-getting-started/event-dashboard) then check/update all the event details and add a logo if desired.

  

**Users**

Click [Users](/01-getting-started/manage-users) in the [Event Dashboard](/01-getting-started/event-dashboard) to invite/remove event administrators, reviewers, committee members and API users.

**Submission, review and decision forms**

Scroll to the **Forms** section of the [Event Dashboard](/01-getting-started/event-dashboard) then click on [Submission form](/02-submissions/), [Review form](http://t.ly/VDMz), [Decision form](/04-decisions/design-the-decision-form) to review/update each of the forms.

  

**Emails**

Click [Emails](/05-emails/) in the [Event Dashboard](/01-getting-started/event-dashboard) then click on **Edit and send** to check each email is fit for purpose and create any custom emails required.

  
  

**Open the call for Submissions**

When you are ready to [open the call for submissions](/02-submissions/opening-and-closing-submissions-deadlines), paste the **Copy submission link** URL from **Overview** section of yourthe [Event Dashboard](/01-getting-started/event-dashboard) into your website and set the submission status to **Open**.
