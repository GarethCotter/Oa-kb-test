# Abstract management checklist

## It is recommended that administrators go through the following checklist to set up the event

### 

**Event details**

Click [Event details](/01-getting-started/event-details) in the [Event Dashboard](https://help.oxfordabstracts.com/_hcms/analytics/search/conversion?redirect=aHR0cHM6Ly9oZWxwLm94Zm9yZGFic3RyYWN0cy5jb20va25vd2xlZGdlL2V2ZW50LWRhc2hib2FyZA%3D%3D&ct=AUTOCOMPLETE&pid=9108826&cid=41194664376&t=ZXZlbnQgZGFzaGJv&d=help.oxfordabstracts.com&c=5&rp=1&ab=true&opcid=fea0d294-2323-4a7f-829b-5211fd032799&rs=LIVE_PAGE&hs-expires=1675423460&hs-version=1&hs-signature=APUk-v7GTHUJDJc20NDyQ6PhXrhr_Xpvqw&hsLang=en) then check/update all the event details and add a logo if desired.

  

**Users**

Click [Users](https://help.oxfordabstracts.com/_hcms/analytics/search/conversion?redirect=aHR0cHM6Ly9oZWxwLm94Zm9yZGFic3RyYWN0cy5jb20va25vd2xlZGdlL21hbmFnZS11c2Vycw%3D%3D&ct=SEARCH&pid=9108826&cid=41196162459&t=dXNlcnM%3D&d=help.oxfordabstracts.com&c=5&rp=1&ab=true&opcid=e344cca7-1525-4c44-bd23-5a88d4b9a261&rs=UNKNOWN&hs-expires=1675424073&hs-version=1&hs-signature=APUk-v72OD_6M_NBzhuM4FZnh6Dl25XMUA&hsLang=en) in the [Event Dashboard](https://help.oxfordabstracts.com/_hcms/analytics/search/conversion?redirect=aHR0cHM6Ly9oZWxwLm94Zm9yZGFic3RyYWN0cy5jb20va25vd2xlZGdlL2V2ZW50LWRhc2hib2FyZA%3D%3D&ct=AUTOCOMPLETE&pid=9108826&cid=41194664376&t=ZXZlbnQgZGFzaGJv&d=help.oxfordabstracts.com&c=5&rp=1&ab=true&opcid=fea0d294-2323-4a7f-829b-5211fd032799&rs=LIVE_PAGE&hs-expires=1675423460&hs-version=1&hs-signature=APUk-v7GTHUJDJc20NDyQ6PhXrhr_Xpvqw&hsLang=en) to invite/remove event administrators, reviewers, committee members and API users.

**Submission, review and decision forms**

Scroll to the **Forms** section of the [Event Dashboard](https://help.oxfordabstracts.com/_hcms/analytics/search/conversion?redirect=aHR0cHM6Ly9oZWxwLm94Zm9yZGFic3RyYWN0cy5jb20va25vd2xlZGdlL2V2ZW50LWRhc2hib2FyZA%3D%3D&ct=AUTOCOMPLETE&pid=9108826&cid=41194664376&t=ZXZlbnQgZGFzaGJv&d=help.oxfordabstracts.com&c=5&rp=1&ab=true&opcid=fea0d294-2323-4a7f-829b-5211fd032799&rs=LIVE_PAGE&hs-expires=1675423460&hs-version=1&hs-signature=APUk-v7GTHUJDJc20NDyQ6PhXrhr_Xpvqw&hsLang=en) then click on [Submission form](/02-submissions/), [Review form](http://t.ly/VDMz), [Decision form](/04-decisions/design-the-decision-form) to review/update each of the forms.

  

**Emails**

Click [Emails](/05-emails/) in the [Event Dashboard](https://help.oxfordabstracts.com/_hcms/analytics/search/conversion?redirect=aHR0cHM6Ly9oZWxwLm94Zm9yZGFic3RyYWN0cy5jb20va25vd2xlZGdlL2V2ZW50LWRhc2hib2FyZA%3D%3D&ct=AUTOCOMPLETE&pid=9108826&cid=41194664376&t=ZXZlbnQgZGFzaGJv&d=help.oxfordabstracts.com&c=5&rp=1&ab=true&opcid=fea0d294-2323-4a7f-829b-5211fd032799&rs=LIVE_PAGE&hs-expires=1675423460&hs-version=1&hs-signature=APUk-v7GTHUJDJc20NDyQ6PhXrhr_Xpvqw&hsLang=en) then click on **Edit and send** to check each email is fit for purpose and create any custom emails required.

  
  

**Open the call for Submissions**

When you are ready to [open the call for submissions](/02-submissions/opening-and-closing-submissions-deadlines), paste the **Copy submission link** URL from **Overview** section of yourthe [Event Dashboard](https://help.oxfordabstracts.com/_hcms/analytics/search/conversion?redirect=aHR0cHM6Ly9oZWxwLm94Zm9yZGFic3RyYWN0cy5jb20va25vd2xlZGdlL2V2ZW50LWRhc2hib2FyZA%3D%3D&ct=AUTOCOMPLETE&pid=9108826&cid=41194664376&t=ZXZlbnQgZGFzaGJv&d=help.oxfordabstracts.com&c=5&rp=1&ab=true&opcid=fea0d294-2323-4a7f-829b-5211fd032799&rs=LIVE_PAGE&hs-expires=1675423460&hs-version=1&hs-signature=APUk-v7GTHUJDJc20NDyQ6PhXrhr_Xpvqw&hsLang=en) into your website and set the submission status to **Open**.
