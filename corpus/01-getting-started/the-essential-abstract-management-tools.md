# The essential abstract management tools

## A brief introduction to the features of the abstract management toolbox, designed to help you manage the submission, review and decision process for your academic or research conference.

#### 

The guidance below is for event administrators/ organisers. If you are an end user (eg. submitter, reviewer, delegate etc), please click [here](https://oxfordabstracts.com/resources/contact-support/).

NB: The features highlighted in pink are NOT available (or are limited) in the FREE package. The FREE package also features **Oxford Abstracts** branding throughout.
