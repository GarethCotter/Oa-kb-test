# Integrating Oxford Abstracts and idloom

## Pull through registration information from idloom into Oxford Abstracts.

On the left-hand menu, click on **Advanced > Integrations > click on the connect button** under IdLoom.

![](/assets/img/undefined-may-12-2026-10-12-05-9026-am-b961e138.webp)

How to generate your idloom API Key.

* Log in to your idloom account
* Open the Integrations section
* Click on **Add Integration**
* Select **API & Webhooks**
* Toggle on 'Allow API access'
* Then click on the Save icon
* Copy your API key

  
  

Now, paste your idloom API Key into the box and click the Find Event button.

  
![](/assets/img/undefined-may-12-2026-10-12-05-6196-am-6f2d1e73.webp)  
  

Now choose the event you want to sync from the list.

Click connect, and the sync should automatically start between your idloom registrations and Oxford Abstracts.

---

**Where to view your idloom registrations**

idloom registrations can be viewed on the Decisions table in Oxford Abstracts. Under the registration column, they will be marked as ‘Registered externally’.
