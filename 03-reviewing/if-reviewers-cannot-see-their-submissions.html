If your reviewers can't see their assigned submissions

A reviewer signs in and finds nothing waiting, or is told they have not been assigned anything. Work down this list — the first two causes account for most of it.#

1. They are signed in with a different email address#

Much the most common cause. A reviewer only sees submissions assigned to the exact address you assigned them under, and people who review for more than one conference often have two addresses.

Ask them to check the address shown in the top right of the screen once signed in, and compare it against the address in Event dashboard → Reviewing → By reviewer. If it is a different address, either add the one they actually use and reassign, or ask them to sign in with the assigned one.

2. They have not registered yet#

Assigning submissions to somebody does not create an account for them. Until they create one, the By reviewer table shows them as unregistered, and they will see nothing.

Ask them to register at app.oxfordabstracts.com using that same address. Their name fills in automatically once the account exists, so the table is a quick way to check who still needs to do this.

3. Reviewing is not open for that stage#

Reviewers only see submissions while reviewing is open. On a multi-stage event, check the specific stage they are assigned to rather than the event as a whole — see Opening and closing reviewing.

4. Their account is linked to Google or LinkedIn#

Signing in through Google or LinkedIn can attach their reviews to a different identity. Unlinking it from their profile page resolves it — see Updating your profile.

5. They are using an email alias#

An alias is not the same address as the mailbox behind it. It needs its own Oxford Abstracts account, and the alias itself has to be on the reviewer list — it does not inherit access from the main address.

6. Every review shows as already complete#

If your review form has no required questions, reviews complete themselves the moment they are assigned, so reviewers open the list and find nothing left to do. Make at least one question required.

7. They see ID numbers instead of titles#

That means the wrong question is set as the title question on your submission form. Set the title question correctly and the titles appear.

If none of that explains it#

Contact support with the reviewer's email address and a screenshot of what they see, including the address in the top right. Removing a reviewer and re-adding them is safe: unassigning does not delete review data, so any review they had already completed shows as completed again once the submission is reassigned.
