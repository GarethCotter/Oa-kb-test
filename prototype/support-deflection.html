What this is demonstrating

  • No confidence percentage. “We’re 90% sure” is false precision, and the one time it is confidently wrong, trust is gone. The wording carries the doubt instead.
  • The send is never blocked. Send my ticket anyway is visible from the first moment, one click, never hidden behind the answer. Someone filling in a support form is already frustrated; making them fight past a robot is the moment that costs loyalty.
  • It only interrupts when the answer is strong. Try the weak answer scenario — the ticket goes straight through with no interruption at all.
  • Failure is silent. Try API unavailable — the reader sees the normal form, never an error.
  • The ticket carries what was tried. Send anyway and look at what reaches support: the suggested articles travel with it.
