# Integrating EventsAir to Oxford Abstracts

## Learn how to pull through registration information from EventsAir into Oxford Abstracts.

On the **left-hand menu**, click on **Advanced > Integrations > click on the connect button** under EventsAir.

  

You’ll **need your EventsAir, Client ID, Client Secret and EventsAir event ID**.

To get this information, [follow this link](https://developer.eventsair.com/docs/guides/create-api-key/) to the EventsAir guide that explains what you need to do.

Once you have this information, enter it into the boxes on the page and click the Connect button.
