Integrating Oxford Abstracts and Swapcard

Integrations & APILast reviewed 28 July 2026

Learn how to export your Oxford Abstract session information and insert it into Swapcard.#

On the left-hand menu, click on Advanced > Integrations > click on the connect button under Swapcard.

You’ll need to add your Swapcard API key to the box and click the connect button.

To find out how to generate your Swapcard API Key, please take a look at this Swapcard guide.
